BRAND IDENTITY, VERSION 1.0 PROPOSAL

AAICBI is an ecosystem, not a course site.

A redesigned identity for Africa’s AI Capacity Building Initiative, organized by Futybills Tech Community. Nested Ascent: one mark that carries the acronym, upward movement, layered institutions and the single node where talent meets opportunity.

2 · THREE DIRECTIONS

Concepts explored

Three different ideas, not three variations. Scores are a design judgement from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong), made by the designer for this proposal; they are not user-tested.

A · Nested Ascent (selected)

Idea. The letter A built from geometry alone: a large chevron, a second chevron inside it, and one node. It reads as A and A (Africa, AI), as upward movement, and as layers (people, institutions, industry).

Risk. A chevron can look like a generic “up” arrow, which the second chevron and the node counter.

Sophistication5Distinctiveness4Scalability5Ecosystem relevance4

B · Open Aperture

Idea. Three arcs around an open centre, suggesting an iris, a lens, intelligence looking outward, with the node at the centre.

Risk. Close to many existing AI and camera marks; says nothing about Africa or the acronym.

Sophistication4Distinctiveness2Scalability4Ecosystem relevance3

C · Node Lattice

Idea. A triangle of connected nodes: people and organizations joined in a network, one amber node for opportunity.

Risk. The “network of dots” is the most common technology cliché, and it breaks down below about 24 px.

Sophistication3Distinctiveness2Scalability2Ecosystem relevance5

Why A. It is the only direction that holds up as a 16 px favicon, works as the letter in the name, and carries the ecosystem idea without an obvious technology icon.

3 · THE MARK

Nested Ascent

Large chevron (Midnight). The institution of the ecosystem: Africa’s AI community as a whole, rising. Its flat apex is a deliberate, print-safe cut rather than a needle point.

Second chevron (Signal Teal). The layer inside: trainees, professionals, startups and organizations, held by the larger structure and moving up with it. Its legs run parallel to the outer ones, at an even gap.

Node (Savanna Amber). The single point where talent meets an opportunity, an employer, an investor or a collaborator. It sits on the shared baseline and is the only warm colour in the identity.

African geometry, used quietly. The triangle and the layered, repeated chevron echo strip-woven and carved patterns found across the continent. They are abstracted, not copied from any single tradition.

6 · TYPE

Typography

AAICBI
AFRICA’S AI CAPACITY BUILDING INITIATIVE

Wordmark. Inter Display Bold, converted to outlines, with +7% letter-spacing. It is neutral, geometric and legible at small sizes. I kept the standard letter A in the wordmark after testing a bar-less A: it was elegant but read as “MICBI” at small sizes.

Descriptor. Inter Display SemiBold capitals, +12% tracking, sized so the line is exactly as wide as the wordmark.

Recommended working fonts. Inter Display for headlines, Inter for text and interfaces (both free under the SIL Open Font License). The platform currently uses Manrope and Fraunces; moving to Inter would be a separate, optional change.

8 · USAGE

Brand usage guide

Clear space

Keep a margin of x on every side, where x is one quarter of the symbol’s height. Nothing enters it.

xxxx

Minimum size

Symbol16 px high5 mm high

Primary logo, stacked130 px wide35 mm wide

Below the primary sizes the full name becomes unreadable: switch to the secondary logo, then to the symbol.

Backgrounds

Colour logo on white, Mist or very light neutrals. Reverse on Midnight or any dark with at least 7 : 1 contrast. White (mono) on a single solid brand colour or a photo with a dark overlay. Black (mono, Midnight) for one-colour print, fax, stamps and embossing.

Incorrect usage

✕ Do not stretch or squash

✕ Do not rotate or tilt

✕ Do not change the colours

✕ Do not add shadows or glows

✕ Do not use low contrast

✕ Do not place on busy images

✕ Do not put the colour logo on mid-tone colour

✕ Do not rearrange, mirror or re-space the parts
