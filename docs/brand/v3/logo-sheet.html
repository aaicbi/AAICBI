AAICBI logo, version 3

The Nested Ascent mark in the platform’s own colours. The mark stands on the word, and together they fill one equilateral triangle.

Tier 1 · Everyday logo

The mark above the word, no descriptor. Every side of the triangle is the same length: the base is the word’s baseline, and the two sides run along the mark’s outer legs. No part of any letter crosses a side (the nearest point, the top corner of the last I, clears it by 0.6 of a unit on a 193-unit-tall logo).

Tier 2 · Formal lockup

The same logo with “Africa’s AI Capacity Building Initiative” beneath the base, for letterheads, certificates and investor decks. It is not used in everyday places.

Compact versions

Below about 120 px tall the letters in the triangle become too small to read. Headers and bars use the compact lockup, with the word beside the mark; the symbol alone is for icons and avatars.

The name

AAICBI is pronounced “Ayak-bi”, close to the Turkish ayakkabı (shoe): what a person wears to protect their feet when they set out on a journey. The platform is built for that journey of self-development. The story belongs on the about page, not on the logo.
